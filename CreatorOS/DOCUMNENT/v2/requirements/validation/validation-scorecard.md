# Validation Scorecard — CreatorOS v2

**Version:** 1.1
**Date:** 2026-08-23
**Purpose:** Go/no-go decision before building full MVP

## 1. Thresholds

| Metric | Required | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Interviewed professional UGC creators | ≥20 | Interview notes |
| Participated in concierge workflow | ≥8 of 10 | Concierge logs |
| Used record for real campaign | ≥8 of 10 | Record data |
| Returned unprompted for next production cycle | ≥5 of 10 | Follow-up session |
| Willing to pay $12–15/month | ≥5 of 10 | **Actual payment or signed pilot with card on file** |
| Qualitative value attribution to cross-tool clarity | ≥6 of 10 | Interview transcripts |
| Connection health perceived as valuable | ≥6 of 10 | Interview transcripts |
| No strong preference for "all-in-one OS" positioning | ≥7 of 10 | Positioning test |

## 2. Scoring Model

Each metric receives 0–3 points:

- 0 = not met
- 1 = partially met
- 2 = met
- 3 = exceeded

## 3. Decision Rules

### GO if:

- No critical metric scores 0.
- Total score ≥ 18 out of 24.
- At least 5 users have paid or committed to a paid pilot.

### NO-GO if:

- More than one critical metric scores 0.
- Total score < 14.
- Users consistently prefer manual workflows or Notion templates.

### PIVOT / ITERATE if:

- Exactly one critical metric scores 0 and total score ≥ 14.
- Mixed results: refine positioning, connector set, or target segment.

## 4. Critical Metrics

1. Participated in concierge workflow
2. Used record for real campaign
3. Returned unprompted
4. Willing to pay $12–15/month (actual payment or signed pilot)

## 5. Final Decision

| Decision | Conditions |
|---|---|
| Proceed to full MVP build | GO criteria met |
| Pivot or iterate | PIVOT criteria met |
| Stop or rethink | NO-GO criteria met |
