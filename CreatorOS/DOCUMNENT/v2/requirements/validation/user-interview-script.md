# User Interview Script — CreatorOS v2 Validation

**Version:** 1.0  
**Date:** 2026-08-23  
**Target:** 20–30 professional UGC creators  
**Goal:** Validate the connected content record problem and willingness to pay before building MVP  

---

## 1. Screening Criteria

Recruit only creators who:

- Do paid UGC or brand deliverables.
- Deliver at least 4 short-form assets per month.
- Use at least 3 of: Google Drive, Google Docs, Google Calendar, Notion, Canva, CapCut.
- Work primarily from mobile.
- Have experienced lost files, missed deadlines, or unclear handoffs in the last 30 days.

---

## 2. Interview Structure

Duration: 30–40 minutes  
Format: remote video call or in-person  
Incentive: $50 or equivalent gift card  

---

## 3. Warm-up & Context

1. Walk me through your current workflow from receiving a brand brief to delivering final content.
2. What tools do you use at each step?
3. How do you know what to work on next when you have multiple clients?

---

## 4. Problem Discovery

4. Tell me about a recent time you could not find the right brief, script, or footage folder.
   - What were you looking for?
   - How long did it take?
   - What was the consequence?

5. Tell me about a time a handoff broke down—export missing, wrong version, expired link, or connection issue.
   - How did you find out?
   - What did you do?

6. How do you currently know if a Google Drive token is expired or a Notion page is stale?
   - How do you discover this?
   - How does it affect your work?

---

## 5. Solution Framing

Do not mention CreatorOS by name initially. Describe the concept:

> "A mobile workspace where you connect Google Drive, Docs, Calendar, and Notion once. For each brand deliverable, you create a record that links the brief, script, footage folder, Canva design, CapCut handoff, and delivery status. It searches across all connected tools, shows you when a connection is broken, and gives you a receipt for every handoff."

7. What is your immediate reaction?
8. Would you use this for your next real campaign?
9. What would make you trust it enough to connect Google Drive and Notion?
10. What actions should require explicit confirmation?
11. What would make you not use it?

---

## 6. Willingness to Pay

12. How much do you currently spend per month on tools for your UGC work?

13. What is the most you would pay for a tool that reliably saves you 30–60 minutes per week and prevents missed deliveries? (Ask without suggesting a price.)

14. Would you be willing to pay for it now if it worked as described?

15. Would you put a card on file for a paid pilot after using it for one real campaign?

(Do not mention $12–15 until after the participant gives an unprompted number.)

---

## 7. Positioning Test

Show two statements:

A. "All-in-one creator OS to replace your stack."  
B. "Connected content record: know which brief, files, design, and edit belong to every post."

16. Which statement feels more relevant to you? Why?
17. Would you be more likely to try product A or B?

---

### 7.1 Switching Cost Probe

18b. If you already use a Notion template or similar system, what would make you switch from it to CreatorOS?

---

## 8. Close

18. Is there anyone else I should talk to?
19. May I follow up with a prototype test?

---

## 9. Interviewer Notes

- Capture direct quotes.
- Record specific tool names and workflow fragments.
- Note any existing Notion template or scheduler they already use.
- Mark whether they independently mention "connection," "stale," or "lost" without prompting.
