## 1. Purpose

This tracker turns the validation plan into an executable project using evidence from recent research.

It defines:
- Where to recruit professional UGC creators and freelance social-media managers
- What incentives to offer and what response rates to expect
- How to run a human-powered concierge prototype
- How to collect actual paid pilot commitments
- Exact go/no-go thresholds for the build decision

All results must be recorded with evidence: source, outreach sent, responses, qualified, attended, started, completed, paid, and decline reasons. No simulated data.

## 2. Research-Based Planning Assumptions

These are planning ranges, not guarantees. Actual rates will be tracked and used to adjust.

| Assumption | Conservative Range | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Cold generic response | 1–3% | Avoid generic outreach |
| Personalized response | 5–12% | Reference a real post/portfolio |
| Personalized + cash incentive | 10–25% optimistic; plan 5–12% | Strong for professionals |
| Community post response | 5–20 raw replies | Only 35–70% qualify |
| Qualified-to-booked | 40–60% | Depends on incentive and scheduling |
| Booked-to-attended | 75–90%; plan 80% | Use reminders and 24h payment promise |
| Attended-to-concierge-start | 60–80% | Require active project |
| Start-to-completion | 60–80% | Use milestone pay |
| Paid-pilot conversion | 20–40% of completers | 3–5 paid from 10–15 value-exposed |

## 3. Target Cohort & Quotas

Recruit **15–18 starters** to finish **10–12 completers**.

Quotas:

| Segment | Minimum |
|---|---|
| UGC creators with 3+ paid projects in last 6 months | 4 |
| Freelance social-media managers with 2+ active clients | 3 |
| Hybrid creator/editor who films, edits, delivers | 3 |
| Agency contractors or direct-client workflows | 2 |
| International participants | 25–35% of cohort |
| Multi-tool fragmented stack users | At least 50% |

Exclude beginners, gifted-only creators, and anyone without recent paid client work.

## 5. Outreach Principles

- Lead with “Paid research,” duration, cash amount, and no-sales disclosure.
- Reference a real portfolio item or post.
- Seek moderator approval before posting.
- Keep screener under 3 minutes.
- One follow-up only after 5–7 days.

## 8. Concierge Prototype Setup

### Tools

- Participant-facing workspace: Notion
- Internal operating system: Airtable
- Intake: Tally or Google Forms
- Communication: Email, WhatsApp, Discord (participant preference)

### Workflow

Week 0:
- 45-min baseline interview
- Select one active client project
- Collect redacted source links
- Record baseline time-to-find a specific brief/asset

Week 1:
- Deliver manual record with Today view, deliverables, source links
- Accept “find this” requests
- Send daily or on-demand status digest
- Deliver at least two useful actions

Week 2:
- Continue creator-initiated requests
- Send connection health check and action receipts
- Present paid continuation offer on day 8–10
- Exit interview

### Manual simulations

- **Cross-tool search:** manual index + respond with source link, excerpt, timestamp, confidence.
- **Connection health:** daily checklist for missing/stale/duplicate sources.
- **Action receipts:** log trigger, evidence, action, state change, next owner, outcome.

## 9. Paid Pilot Setup

### Offer message

> Your 7-day CreatorOS pilot ends on [date]. During it, we helped you [specific outcome].  
> Continue for 30 days: **$12/month** or **$15/month** founding beta. Includes manual support and clear scope: [terms].  
> Continue: [Stripe Payment Link]

Use a one-time 30-day payment first. Ask for deliberate renewal at day 25.

### Payment tools

- Stripe Payment Links for one-time 30-day beta.
- No card-on-file required initially.
- Track payment status, offer price, date, renewal.

### Strong evidence thresholds

| Signal | Weak | Promising | Strong |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starts 7-day concierge | <30% | 30–50% | >50% |
| Completes and pays | <15% | 20–35% | >35% |
| Month-2 renewal | <40% | 50–65% | >65% |

Target: **3–5 paid pilots from 10–15 value-exposed creators**, with at least 50% deliberate renewal.

## 13. Go/No-Go Criteria

Link to `validation-scorecard.md`, with these operational thresholds:

- **GO:** At least 8 of 12 completers use the record twice; at least 5 proactive requests; at least 3 actual paid pilots; at least 50% renewal among payers.
- **PIVOT:** Some use but no payment; search valued but health ignored; buyer is client/agency, not creator.
- **NO-GO:** Fewer than half of starters complete; fewer than 30% proactive use; zero paid commitments.

## 14. Risks and Mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Low response rate | Prioritize referrals, moderator-approved posts, warm LinkedIn |
| Mostly beginners apply | Strict screener; verify paid work |
| No-shows | 20% overbooking, reminders, standby list |
| Creator won't pay | Offer after 2 value events; ask buyer source; record decline reason |
| Concierge feels like software demo | Be transparent it's manual |
| Privacy concerns | No passwords, no client-confidential files, redaction allowed |
| International payouts | Local gift cards, cash transfer options |
